About Me

HR professional with a data-driven approach.

I’m Confidence Edafe, ACIPM — an HR professional with 4 years of experience across HR operations, employee data, reporting, and workforce analysis.

My work sits at the intersection of HR and data. I help organise employee information, build practical reporting systems, and turn HR data into clear insights that support better workforce decisions.

I believe good HR reporting is not just about presenting numbers. It is about understanding the story behind the numbers, putting them in business context, and identifying what needs attention.

My approach is simple: Data → Insight → Action.

I work with tools including Excel, Power BI, Google Sheets, PowerPoint, and HRIS platforms, while continuing to develop my skills in SQL, Tableau, and Python.
